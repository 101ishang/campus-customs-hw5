No payment may be made without explicit human approval, and no action may create a negative cash balance; if approved, the relevant database records must be updated through the guarded MCP payment workflow.

Keep the work limited to the supplied ticket and a small number of MCP calls. Return an evidence-backed status, dependencies, and a draft notice for the board rather than claiming that any real-world communication occurred.
